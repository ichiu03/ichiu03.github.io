# Vision

Owned by me and the orchestrator. Agents read it; only the orchestrator edits it, with my approval.

## Why this exists
I graduated from Boston College in May 2026 (CS) and I'm looking for software engineering roles.
A recruiter or hiring manager should finish one visit knowing three things: I ship real products
to real users, I build across the whole stack and into AI, and how to reach me.

## What success looks like
- Experience and projects read as short stories: the problem, what I built, the result. Lead
  with the strongest: Fidelity's "Saved Orders" (10M+ users), the agentic LLM pipeline at
  Decodables, and the Claritas fullstack work.
- Everything on my resume is on the site itself; there's no downloadable PDF.
- Contact is an email button plus GitHub (github.com/ichiu03) and LinkedIn
  (linkedin.com/in/ivan-chiu-32b87527b) links. No form, no backend.
- It feels like me and is memorable, not a template.
- It's live on GitHub Pages at `<username>.github.io`.

## Goals
- Redesign around the theme below.
- Deploy to GitHub Pages as a static export.
- Add a "how I build with AI agents" angle, tying in the Decodables pipeline and my Claude Code
  workflow. It should set me apart.

## Non-goals
- A blog or CMS.
- A backend, database, or server features.
- A heavy test suite. Build, lint, and a few smoke checks are enough.

## Anti-goals
- Hard to read. The theme must never make my experience hard to skim.
- Too feminine or too cute. Sakura is an accent, not the whole palette.
- Anime characters or anything that reads as a fan site.
- Facts that don't match my resume, or my phone number on the page.

## Taste and principles
- **Mood:** pixel-art Japan at dusk, like a lo-fi anime game screen. Subtle, not costume-y.
- **Colors:** dark ink and indigo base; sakura pink and vermilion (torii red) as accents only.
- **Fonts:** VT323 (the pixel font from krillion.io) for headings, nav and accents; a clean,
  readable sans (e.g. Inter or IBM Plex Sans) for body text.
- **Motifs:** falling pixel petals, a small torii or moon. Motion stays light and respects
  reduced-motion settings.
- Fast, responsive, and readable on a phone.
